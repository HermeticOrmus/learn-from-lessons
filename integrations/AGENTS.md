## Lessons from past sessions

`lessons` (learn-from-lessons) holds lessons from this person's earlier agent sessions, each citing the turns it
came from. They are predictions: follow them unless the person says otherwise now (then mention the conflict).

- Principles (the few general ones) are in the generated block below; they always apply.
- Before a risky or recurring step — pushing or opening a PR, spending API credit, writing paper prose, reporting
  results — search with the current scenario:
  `lessons search "<what you are about to do>" --cwd --context "<the request>"` (or `--scenario <id>`).
  Follow the **Do** of any hit whose trigger fits; `--all` adds unverified candidates (hints only).
- With the MCP server: `search_lessons(query, scenario)`, `get_lesson(id)`, `list_principles()`, `infer_scenario(text)`.
- No lessons yet, or a correction keeps recurring: suggest `lessons dream` (it calls a model, so ask first).
- The block between `<!-- learn-from-lessons:start -->` and `<!-- learn-from-lessons:end -->` is generated by
  `lessons inject`; do not edit it by hand.
